About Us

Inspired by the noble visions of Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam and Swami Vivekananda, our founder, a devoted follower of their teachings, established Prapanja Foundation with a twofold mission: to empower future generations through education and uplift the underprivileged sections of society.

Prapanja stands as a humble contributor in the grand mission of building tomorrow's leaders. We believe that great leaders continue to inspire through their ideas and aspirations. Our mission is to keep those ideals alive and transform them into meaningful action that creates opportunities, confidence, and hope for future generations.

Founder's Message

Dr. Abdul Kalam, a visionary leader and my lifelong inspiration, instilled in me a profound belief in the transformative power of education. Witnessing my own journey from humble beginnings to becoming a Chartered Accountant, I realized the importance of creating opportunities for those who lack access to quality education and guidance.

Prapanja Foundation is my tribute to Dr. Kalam and his dream of a developed India. We believe education is not merely about academic success; it is about empowering individuals to build meaningful lives and contribute positively to society.

Our mission goes beyond helping students secure admission into reputed institutions. We provide mentorship, financial support, career guidance, and life skills that help them navigate the world with confidence.

Prapanja also strives to support women and transgender communities by creating opportunities that promote independence, dignity, and personal growth.

We are still young, but our vision is strong and our commitment unwavering. Together, with the support of volunteers, mentors, and well-wishers, we can build a future where every child and every individual has the opportunity to dream, learn, and succeed.

With gratitude and hope,

Founder

I to I Programme

Empowering Students from Every Background

For students from families with limited educational and professional awareness, navigating the path to success can be challenging. Many lack access to information about career opportunities, educational pathways, and professional development.

Our I to I Programme acts as a bridge that connects students with the guidance, mentorship, and resources needed to unlock their full potential. Through personalized support and expert mentoring, we help students gain confidence, make informed decisions, and build a brighter future.
